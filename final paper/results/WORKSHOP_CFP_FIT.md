# Workshop CFP Fit

## Official-status constraint

No public 2026 workshop list or workshop-specific CFP pages were located on the official NeurIPS sources inspected in this audit. This fit memo therefore targets workshop theme families rather than named workshops.

## Best-fit theme families

### 1. Multi-agent RL / cooperative AI

Why it fits:
- The core problem is held-out partner coordination.
- The baselines are standard MARL symmetry baselines.
- The main insight is about when a symmetry bias helps or hurts teamwork.

### 2. RL foundations / generalization / robustness

Why it fits:
- The paper is fundamentally about a mismatch between a mechanistic diagnostic (permutation sensitivity) and a downstream generalization outcome (held-out partner ZSC).
- The contribution is as much diagnostic as algorithmic.

### 3. Empirical limitations / diagnostics in modern ML

Why it fits:
- The paper shows that improving a natural intermediate metric does not guarantee improvement on the task-level objective.
- The new mismatch figure turns mixed results into a reusable empirical lesson.

## Minimal adaptation needed once a real CFP is public

- If the workshop is double-blind, switch from `preprint` to the appropriate anonymous NeurIPS workshop option and set the workshop title.
- If the workshop is single-blind or camera-ready, keep the current non-anonymous structure and only update the footnote/workshop title.
- If the workshop has a shorter page limit than the generic NeurIPS format, move one of the main-paper diagnostic figures to the appendix first.
