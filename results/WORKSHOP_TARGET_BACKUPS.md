# Workshop Target Backups

Because no official 2026 workshop list or CFP pages were public in the inspected official sources, the backups are theme families rather than named workshops.

## Backup 1

RL foundations / generalization / robustness

Why it fits:
- The central result is about the failure of a natural invariance diagnostic to predict downstream generalization to held-out partners.
- The paper is fundamentally an empirical study of what a symmetry bias does and does not buy.

Empirical ML diagnostics / inductive bias analysis

Why it fits:
- The paper turns a mixed method result into a concrete diagnostic lesson.
- The new mismatch figure is useful even beyond MARL: better invariance metrics do not automatically imply better task outcomes.

## Backup 3

Small-scale but careful cooperative AI / ad hoc teamwork

Why it fits:
